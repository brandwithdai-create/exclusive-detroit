- the background skyline in the Tonight section
- the background skyline after entering Build My Night

The title weight got too heavy.

- make “Build My Night” thinner and more elegant again
- closer to the refined typography used in the hero section

We want:
high-end hospitality branding energy — not chunky UI typography.

Direction:

IMPORTANT:
This applies BOTH to:

Everything in this light mode itinerary flow should feel cohesive.
