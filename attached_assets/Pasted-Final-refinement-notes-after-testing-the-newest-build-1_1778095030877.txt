Final refinement notes after testing the newest build:

1. PASSPORT TAB — REMOVE THE FLOATING COLOR AURA
The passport stamps themselves look much better now, but there’s still colored haze/glow floating around the stamps.

Please remove ALL of the soft color fog/background glow behind the passport stamps.

The stamps should look:
- clean
- inked
- printed directly onto the passport page

Keep ONLY:
- the stamp outline
- the venue illustration
- the venue name
- neighborhood
- visited date

No:
- colored cloud
- glow bloom
- soft blur aura
- floating gradient behind stamps

Also:
- rotate the stamps slightly at different angles
- make them look naturally imperfect
- slightly offset some placements
- let them feel physically stamped into a real passport over time

The goal is:
vintage luxury passport aesthetic — not floating UI stickers.

2. ITINERARY SCREEN — LIGHT MODE IS TOO DARK
The redesign direction is correct, but the light mode styling became way too dark.

Please rebalance it back toward the app’s luxury ivory/light aesthetic.

What to change:
- reduce the heavy black overlays
- soften the darkness significantly
- bring back warm ivory + cream tones
- use elegant translucent glass panels instead of deep black cards
- keep the skyline cinematic but lighter and more refined

It should feel like:
- luxury hotel concierge
- editorial luxury travel
- elevated lifestyle guide

NOT:
- gaming UI
- nightclub dashboard
- cyberpunk

3. SKYLINE BACKGROUND SHOULD EXTEND TO THE BOTTOM
Right now the skyline image cuts off too early and leaves a plain section underneath.

The Detroit skyline background/photo should continue all the way down the full Build My Night section.

Specifically:
- extend the skyline image to the bottom of the itinerary experience
- remove the abrupt cutoff
- keep the cinematic Detroit background visible behind the entire experience
- make it feel immersive and continuous

4. MAP — MAKE THE ENTIRE VENUE CARD CLICKABLE
The new map positioning is much better now.

One final UX fix:
the venue card itself is too small to tap precisely.

Please make the ENTIRE floating venue card clickable:
- image
- title
- black card background
- text area
- entire component

Tapping anywhere on the venue card should open the venue details.

Do not require users to tap only “View Details.”
The whole card should behave as one large tap target.
