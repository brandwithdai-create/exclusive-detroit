6. Fix Tonight / Build My Night strict filtering

This is very important. The itinerary cannot show venues that do not match what the user selected.

Current issue:
Morning was selected, but London Chop House and Candy Bar appeared. That should never happen.

Rules:
- Morning = breakfast, brunch, coffee, bakeries only.
- Afternoon = lunch, cafés, daytime activities, and happy hour only if actually tagged happy hour.
- Evening = dinner first, then cocktails/lounge/experience.
- Late Night = late-night food/drinks only, based on actual late-night tags/hours.
- Happy Hour = only venues that actually have happy hour.
- Date Night / 2 of Us + Elevated = upscale, romantic, cute, polished spots. Restaurant first, then lounge/cocktail/after spot.
- Calm = quiet, intimate, low-noise, relaxed spots.
- Chill = casual, easygoing, sports bars, dive bars, low-pressure neighborhood spots.
- Elevated = luxury, upscale, date-night, hotel lounge, refined experiences.
- High Energy = nightlife, party, lively, busy/bumping spots.

Do not use a broad fallback that breaks user intent.

If there are not enough matching venues, show fewer results or say something like:
“Not enough matching spots for this combination. Try another time or vibe.”

Do not show wrong places just to fill three cards.
