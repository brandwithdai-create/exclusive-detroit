The refinement is moving in the right direction, but the Passport and “Choose for Me” sections are not there yet. Please do a targeted correction pass.

1. Energy options need to change
   Replace the current energy options with:

- Calm
- Chill
- Elevated
- High Energy

Meaning:

- Calm = quiet, intimate, low-noise, relaxed spots
- Chill = casual, low-pressure, sports bars, dive bars, easygoing spots
- Elevated = luxury, polished, date-night, upscale, hotel/lounge, refined experiences
- High Energy = nightlife, party, lively bars, places that are usually busy/bumping

Do not assign venues randomly. The itinerary logic must match what the user selected.

2. Build My Night must be smarter
   If the user selects Date Night or 2 of Us + Elevated, the app should suggest cute/upscale date-friendly places: dinner first, then a polished lounge/cocktail spot, then dessert/after spot if available.

Examples of the type of venues that fit Date Night/Elevated:

- Candy Bar
- Prime + Proper
- Parc
- Sexy Steak
- Grey ghost
- Highlands / High Bar
- The Monarch Club
- Bad Luck Bar
- Standby
- The Siren / Ash Bar if already in the venue data

Use only existing venue data. If a venue is not already in the static data, do not invent it. If the current data does not have enough tags to know what fits, add minimal safe tags manually to existing venue objects such as:

- calm
- chill
- elevated
- highEnergy
- dateNight
- food
- drinks
- lateNight
- brunch
- groupFriendly

The algorithm should always choose based on these tags, not random category fallback.

3. Choose for Me needs to feel fun and app-like
   The current “Choose for Me” card is too plain. Make it feel interactive and fun while staying luxury.

Add a subtle animation, like:

- rolling dice
- shuffling cards
- spinning selector
- “curating your night…” transition

Keep it refined, not childish. The button should feel exciting, not like a plain form button.

When the user taps Choose for Me:

- show a short animation
- then reveal a smart suggestion based on time of day
- always include food unless the suggestion is specifically drinks-only
- avoid breakfast after breakfast hours
- lunch during midday
- dinner + cocktails/lounge in the evening
- late-night food/drinks late at night

Reference style:

- irregular travel stamp shapes

- thin ink-like borders

- each stamp should look different

- no giant bubble layout

The Passport should feel like a luxury travel passport page, not badges inside bubbles.
