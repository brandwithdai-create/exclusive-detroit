Please do one final iPad stress/random behavior test before Apple resubmission.

Goal: confirm the app does not crash, freeze, show a blank screen, or become unresponsive under real Apple-review-style behavior.

Test on iPad sizes:
- iPad Air 11-inch portrait
- iPad Air 11-inch landscape
- iPad Pro 11-inch portrait
- Standard iPad portrait

Please test:

1. Cold launch stress
- fully close app
- reopen app
- repeat multiple times
- confirm no blank screen and no crash

2. Network stress
- open with normal internet
- open with slow internet
- briefly disconnect internet during launch
- reconnect internet
- open app with no internet if possible
- confirm app shows visible UI/fallback and does not crash

3. Rapid tap stress
- quickly tap bottom tabs back and forth
- Explore → Map → Saved → About → back again
- rapidly open and close venue detail modals
- rapidly save/unsave hearts
- rapidly change map filters
- confirm no frozen state or console errors

4. Orientation stress
- open in portrait
- rotate to landscape
- rotate back to portrait
- open map
- open venue detail
- rotate again
- confirm layout does not collapse and map stays usable

5. Map stress
- pan map
- zoom map
- tap multiple pins
- open and close map detail cards
- switch categories while a card is open
- confirm Leaflet does not crash

6. Image stress
- open multiple venue cards quickly
- close and reopen the same card
- scroll fast through Explore
- open Hotels, Things To Do, and About
- confirm images load fast, no blank image boxes, no broken images

7. Background/resume stress
- open app
- send it to background
- reopen after a moment
- switch tabs
- confirm no blank screen or frozen UI

8. Final check
Please report:
- exact iPad viewports tested
- whether any bug/crash/freeze happened
- whether offline/slow internet was tested
- whether any console errors appeared
- whether any files were changed
- confirmation production build is still live and clean

Do not change design, data, links, categories, APIs, or layout unless a real bug is found.
